Tel Aviv — per-bike trails, labeled with businesses

Public data.lime.bike GBFS free_bike_status snapshots (dense ~15 s). Each persistent bike_id leaves the feed when a rental starts and reappears under the same id when it ends. Any bike_id ever seen in the public feed renders its own labeled trail: street map, per-endpoint nearest venue, and a chronological timeline. Headline statistics are confirmed-only (classifier). Vehicle-level/statistical output only — no person-identifying inference.
