Cross-epoch identity chains

Lime rotates the whole bike_id namespace in batches. Chaining is the spatial re-identification of the same physical vehicle across a rotation: identical position (<30 m, 1e-5 grid) and identical current_range_meters. This is what defeats per-trip rotation.
